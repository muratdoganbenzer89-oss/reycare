The ritual

Three steps, morning and night

Apply from the lightest texture to the richest, so each layer can absorb properly.

STEP 1

Serum

Apply 2–3 drops to clean skin and press gently with your fingertips.

STEP 2

Moisturize

Once the serum has absorbed, massage a pea-sized amount upwards over face and neck.

STEP 3

Body care

After showering, massage the Anti-Stretch Mark Cream into damp skin in circular motions.

FAQ

Good to know

Which skin types are Reycare products suitable for?

Our formulas are designed for normal, dry and combination skin. If you have sensitive skin, try a small amount behind your ear first.

When will I see results?

Skin renews itself roughly every four weeks, so we recommend using the products consistently for at least four weeks. Results vary from person to person.

Can I use the Anti-Stretch Mark Cream during pregnancy?

Please talk to your doctor before starting any new product during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Where can I buy Reycare?

Our official website is the main place to buy Reycare. We are also planning a selective presence in premium beauty retailers and luxury spas.

Which payment methods do you accept?

On a live store we would accept credit cards, bank transfer (EFT) and PayPal. Because this site is a demo, no payment is collected.
